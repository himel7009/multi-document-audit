Vendor Services Agreement — Northwind Logistics Inc.

Section 4: Term and Termination

This Agreement shall remain in effect for an initial term of 24 months from the Effective Date. Either party
may terminate this Agreement for convenience with 90 days written notice. In the event of a material breach,
the non-breaching party may terminate immediately upon written notice, provided the breaching party is given
a 15-day cure period after notice of breach.

Section 5: Payment Terms

Client shall pay all invoices within 45 days of receipt. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.
Vendor may suspend services if an invoice remains unpaid for more than 60 days.

Section 6: Liability Cap

Vendor's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client in the preceding
12 months.
