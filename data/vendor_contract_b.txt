Master Services Agreement — Cascade Analytics LLC.

Section 4: Term and Termination

This Agreement shall remain in effect for an initial term of 12 months from the Effective Date, renewing
automatically for successive 12-month terms unless either party gives 30 days notice of non-renewal. Either
party may terminate for convenience with 60 days written notice. In the event of a material breach, the
non-breaching party may terminate immediately upon written notice, provided the breaching party is given a
30-day cure period after notice of breach.

Section 5: Payment Terms

Client shall pay all invoices within 30 days of receipt. Late payments accrue interest at 2% per month.
Vendor may suspend services if an invoice remains unpaid for more than 45 days.

Section 6: Liability Cap

Vendor's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed two times the total fees paid by Client in the
preceding 12 months.
